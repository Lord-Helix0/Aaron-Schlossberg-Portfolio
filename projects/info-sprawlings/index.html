Origin and evolution

From Folders to a Knowledge Web

Info Sprawlings began as an enormous private research system. Its present form grew from that archive—and from learning why breadth alone was not enough.

Around 2020–2021, I began making Google Drive folders and documents for almost anything I wanted to understand: people, scientific ideas, places, organisms, technologies, philosophies, religions, fictional concepts, and much more. The surviving archive contains more than 275 Word documents, ranging from substantial research to templates, topic queues, and unfinished beginnings.

For a time, I imagined Info Sprawlings and Mayvene beneath an umbrella idea called Knowledge Corp. Then someone described Info Sprawlings as a “glorified Wikipedia.” The criticism exposed a real problem: I had imagined extraordinary breadth without yet defining what would make the project distinctive, sustainable, or particularly mine. I gradually stopped working on it.

Years later, I revived it with a narrower purpose. Info Sprawlings is no longer an attempt to document everything. It is a selective, evolving web of subjects I genuinely want to research, explain, and connect; with context, structured data, related writings, and links to the rest of my work.

Then and now

The idea did not disappear. It changed shape.

Original ambition
Build an enormous archive spanning almost every kind of subject.

Current direction
