Writing Across Forms

I write fiction stories, essays, scripts, technical explanations, website and social content, and general world-building. It has grown through creative writing courses, Computer Science and entrepreneurship, my social media internship with Muhlenberg College’s English Literatures & Creative Writing department, and independent ventures of mine such as Fiction2Reality.

Across those formats, I care about clarity, voice, structure, and revising, whether I am building a fictional world, explaining a complicated idea, or helping an ambitious project communicate what it is trying to become.
