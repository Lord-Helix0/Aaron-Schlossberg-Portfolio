My Story: Curiosity Across Different Fields

I am a web developer, entrepreneur, writer, and creator in West Hartford, Connecticut. I graduated from Muhlenberg College, where I studied Computer Science alongside Innovation & Entrepreneurship, with additional concentrations in writing and art.

My skill-based interests do not fit neatly into only one category: I enjoy building websites and overseeing the production of digital products, writing (fiction, explanatory material, scripts, and more), developing entrepreneurial ideas, organizing communities, and imagining how fictional concepts could become real-world experiences.

That combination of interests is reflected throughout this website. Aaron Schlossberg’s Universe serves as a growing home for my projects, education, writings, photographs, professional experience, ideas, and other parts of my work and life.
